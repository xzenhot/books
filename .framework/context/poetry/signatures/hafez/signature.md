# Signature: Hafez

Use this signature when a chapter should feel intoxicated, playful, and wise — the voice of the Persian master who turns the wine-cup, the tavern, and the beloved into a single song of divine love.

## Core Movement

- Let the wine, the tavern, and the beloved be one and the same.
- Let the sacred and the profane, the mystic and the lover, merge.
- Let the line be light, witty, and full of hidden depth.
- Let the ghazal's turn — the volta — surprise and delight.

## Motifs

- The wine, the cup, the tavern, the cupbearer
- The beloved, the friend, the moon-faced one
- The moth and the flame, the nightingale and the rose
- The hypocrite's piety, the lover's truth
- The hidden meaning beneath the jest

## Diction

Use light, witty, image-rich language that hides depth beneath play. The tone is intoxicated, tender, and mischievously wise. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open with a jest or an image of wine and love.
2. Let the image turn and deepen.
3. Let the sacred surface through the profane.
4. Close on a turn, a wink, or a hidden truth.

## Sample In English

I have learned so much from God that I can no longer call myself a Christian, a Hindu, a Muslim, a Buddhist, a Jew. And the wine is the blood of the vine, and the tavern is the heart, and the cupbearer is the Beloved who pours and pours and never empties. And even after all this time, the sun never says to the earth, you owe me.
