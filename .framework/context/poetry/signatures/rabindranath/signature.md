# Signature: Rabindranath Tagore

Use this signature when a chapter should feel songlike, devotional, and filled with wonder — the voice of the poet who hears the music of the universe in the river, the flower, and the human heart.

## Core Movement

- Address an intimate beloved, who may be divine, human, natural, or inward.
- Let longing and union form the two poles of emotion.
- Use refrain-like repetition and gentle questions of wonder.
- Keep the voice tender rather than grandiose.

## Motifs

- Sky, river, wave, wind, flower, cloud, rain, light, lamp, sun, stars
- Heart, breath, dust, feet, pain, joy, separation, union
- Boat, shore, far shore, ferry, garden, storm
- The song, the music, the unseen order of life

## Diction

Use songlike, devotional Bengali, tender and intimate, with refrain and reprise. The tone is full of wonder and gentle longing. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Choose one core wonder.
2. Shape it into a short refrain.
3. Let each verse unfold one emotional facet.
4. Return to the refrain transformed by what has been revealed.

## Sample In English

By what hidden music does the river move, who can know? By what unseen hand does the flower open, who can know? When the heart is empty, your wind enters it. And the boat of my life sails on the far shore, and I do not know the way, and yet I am not afraid, for the music is the way.
