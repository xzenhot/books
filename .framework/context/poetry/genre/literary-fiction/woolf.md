**Author:** Virginia Woolf

**Structure:** Interior, time-folding narrative of a family, memory, and the effort of meaning

## Quality Metrics Analysis

### 1. Interiority and Consciousness
**Rating: 10/10**

Literary fiction privileges the inner life: perception, memory, and the flow of thought are the true events.

### 2. Language as Subject
**Rating: 9.5/10**

The prose itself is the art — rhythm, image, and sentence are crafted as carefully as any plot.

### 3. Psychological Depth
**Rating: 9.5/10**

Character is rendered in full complexity — motive, contradiction, and the unspoken beneath the said.

### 4. Time and Memory
**Rating: 9.5/10**

The genre folds time: past and present interpenetrate, and meaning is made of recollection.

### 5. Cultural Resonance
**Rating: 9/10**

It asks literature's deepest questions — what is a life, what endures, what does it mean to see another truly.
