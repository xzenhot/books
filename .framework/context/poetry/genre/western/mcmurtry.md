# Book Analysis: Lonesome Dove

## Document Overview
**Source:** mcmurtry.txt
**Work:** Lonesome Dove (1985)
**Author:** Larry McMurtry
**Genre:** Western
**Structure:** Epic cattle-drive narrative across a vast, unforgiving frontier

---

## Quality Metrics Analysis

### 1. The Frontier as Character
**Rating: 9.5/10**

The western's landscape is not backdrop but force — the vast, indifferent land that tests, defines, and outlasts every person in it.

### 2. The Code of the Individual
**Rating: 9/10**

The genre celebrates the self-reliant figure — the cowboy, the drifter — bound by an unspoken code of honor and endurance.

### 3. Freedom and Its Cost
**Rating: 9/10**

The open range is freedom made physical, but the genre is honest about what that freedom costs — loneliness, violence, loss.

### 4. Community Against the Wild
**Rating: 8.5/10**

Small, fragile communities — the town, the outfit — hold together against a hostile world.

### 5. Cultural Resonance
**Rating: 9/10**

It is the national myth of the open horizon: the dream of beginning again, and the price of the dream.
