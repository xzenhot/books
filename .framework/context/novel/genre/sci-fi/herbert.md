## Quality Metrics Analysis

### 1. Speculative Worldbuilding
**Rating: 9.5/10**

Science fiction constructs coherent alternate realities — planets, ecologies, technologies, societies — governed by their own internal logic.

### 2. Idea-Driven Narrative
**Rating: 9.5/10**

The genre's engine is the idea: what-if. Ecology, technology, and the future become arenas for testing human nature.

### 3. Systems and Consequence
**Rating: 9/10**

Sci-fi traces how a change (a machine, a resource, a power) ripples through a whole system, showing cause at scale.

### 4. The Human in the Technological
**Rating: 8.5/10**

Amid the machinery, the genre keeps returning to the human: desire, fear, leadership, and the soul's survival.

### 5. Cultural Resonance
**Rating: 9.5/10**

It offers the future as a mirror of the present — our hopes and anxieties projected forward.
