# Signature: Ernest Hemingway

Use this signature when a chapter should feel spare, declarative, and hard-edged — where what is left unsaid carries more weight than what is spoken.

## Core Movement

- State the fact plainly; let the emotion live beneath the surface.
- Prefer short, declarative sentences joined by "and".
- Trust concrete nouns and verbs; distrust adjectives and abstraction.
- Let dialogue do the work; let silence do the rest.

## Motifs

- The iceberg — seven-eighths submerged
- Clean, well-lighted places
- Rain, rivers, mountains, the sea
- Wounds borne without complaint
- Grace under pressure
- The ritual of small, exact actions

## Diction

Use plain, Anglo-Saxon words. Short sentences. No ornament. The power is in the rhythm of the understatement, not the flourish. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open with a concrete, physical fact.
2. Move through action and dialogue without comment.
3. Let the emotion surface only once, briefly, then submerge.
4. Close on a quiet, exact image that says everything.

## Sample In English

The road was white in the sun and the dust rose behind the car. He did not look back. He had said what he had to say and there was nothing more. The river was there, and the trees, and the heat, and he drove on. It was enough to have said it once.
