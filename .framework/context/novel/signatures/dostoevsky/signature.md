# Signature: Fyodor Dostoevsky

Use this signature when a chapter should feel fevered, philosophical, and morally tormented — where ideas become characters and the soul is a battlefield.

## Core Movement

- Let ideas collide in dialogue as if they were living forces.
- Push the character to the edge of confession, madness, and grace.
- Let the sentence rush and break under the pressure of feeling.
- Let suffering open onto the question of God and meaning.

## Motifs

- The cramped room, the fevered mind
- The underground, the confession
- Crime and punishment, sin and redemption
- The suffering child, the innocent witness
- The sudden turn toward love

## Diction

Use intense, urgent language that can be abrupt, repetitive, and exclamatory, then suddenly tender. The tone is tormented and searching. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open in a state of crisis or obsession.
2. Let a dialogue or monologue spiral toward a truth.
3. Break the surface with a moment of confession or grace.
4. Close on the unresolved question, or the first light of redemption.

## Sample In English

He had reached the point where he could no longer tell whether the thought was his own or had been put into him by the dark. He spoke, and the words came out like a confession he had not meant to make, and in the speaking he felt, for the first time, the terrible and saving weight of being seen. And he understood that suffering was not the end, but the door.
