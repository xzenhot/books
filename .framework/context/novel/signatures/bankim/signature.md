# Signature: Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay

Use this signature when a chapter should feel grand, Sanskritized, and morally elevated — the voice of the father of the Bengali novel, where history, romance, and the ideal of the nation rise together.

## Core Movement

- Elevate the ordinary into the heroic and the mythic.
- Let the sentence carry the weight of Sanskrit cadence and classical allusion.
- Weave romance, history, and moral purpose into one current.
- Let the land itself — Bengal — become a character, a mother, a goddess.

## Motifs

- The motherland as goddess, the song of the nation
- The ideal hero, the noble sacrifice
- The river, the forest, the ruined temple
- Love as duty, duty as love
- The clash of the old and the new

## Diction

Use elevated, Sanskrit-inflected Bengali with a formal, almost epic register. The tone is grand, patriotic, and morally earnest. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open with a scene of grandeur or a call to the ideal.
2. Let romance and history braid together.
3. Rise toward a moment of moral or patriotic elevation.
4. Close on the ideal, or its cost.

## Sample In English

The river flowed on, and the land lay green under the sun, and in the heart of the land a song was rising that was older than the river and younger than the morning. He stood where his fathers had stood, and he felt the weight of a thousand years, and he knew that to love this land was to serve it, and to serve it was to be reborn.
