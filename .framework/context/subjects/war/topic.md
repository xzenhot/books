# Topic: War

## Essence

War is the crucible of the human heart — the collision of nations, of wills, of the hunger for power and the fear of loss. It reveals both the cruelty and the compassion of which we are capable, and it asks the oldest question: what is worth killing for, and what is worth dying for?

## Facets

- **The cause** — pride, fear, greed, grievance, the ancient wound.
- **The battle** — the clash of armies, the chaos of combat, the transformation of the ordinary into the soldier.
- **The cost** — the dead, the wounded, the displaced, the grief that outlives every war.
- **The compassion** — the mercy, the courage, the humanity that refuses to be extinguished.
- **The peace** — the search for reconciliation, the long road home.

## Motifs

- The sword and the shield
- The field of the dead
- The smoke and the fire
- The broken oath, the lost kingdom
- The dove and the olive branch

## Questions

- What does war reveal about the human heart?
- Is there such a thing as a just war?
- Can the wounds of war ever truly heal?
- What is the difference between a soldier and a killer?

## Sample Opening

And an old soldier, whose hands remembered the weight of the sword, said: Speak to us of War. And he answered, saying: War is the shadow that falls across the heart of man, and in its darkness we see both the beast and the angel that dwell within us.
