# Topic: Hope

## Essence

Hope is the light that burns in the dark — the refusal of the heart to accept the night as final. It is not the certainty of the outcome, but the courage to keep walking toward it, the seed that trusts the spring even in the winter.

## Facets

- **The light** — the glimmer in the darkness.
- **The courage** — the strength to keep going.
- **The trust** — the faith in what is not yet seen.
- **The seed** — the promise of the future in the present.
- **The test** — hope against despair, light against the night.

## Motifs

- The candle in the dark
- The seed in the winter earth
- The dawn after the long night
- The star that guides
- The green shoot through the snow

## Questions

- Is hope a virtue or an illusion?
- Can we live without hope?
- What is the difference between hope and wish?
- Does hope make us strong, or merely patient?

## Sample Opening

And a woman, whose days were dark and whose heart still burned, said: Speak to us of Hope. And he answered, saying: Hope is the seed that trusts the spring while the snow still lies upon it. It does not see the sun, yet it reaches toward it, and in the reaching it is already growing.
