# Topic: Sacrifice

## Essence

Sacrifice is the giving of the self for the sake of the other — the laying down of what is dear for what is dearer, the pouring out of the self that another might live. It is the highest act of love, and the measure of what a soul holds sacred.

## Facets

- **The giving** — the self laid down, the gift of what is dear.
- **The cause** — the thing for which the self is given.
- **The cost** — the loss borne, the price paid.
- **The meaning** — the worth the sacrifice confers.
- **The memory** — the sacrifice remembered, the debt honored.

## Motifs

- The seed that dies to live
- The candle that burns to give light
- The lamb and the altar
- The soldier and the field
- The mother and the child

## Questions

- What is worth sacrificing for?
- Is sacrifice a loss or a gain?
- Can a sacrifice be wasted?
- What do we owe those who sacrificed for us?

## Sample Opening

And a mother, whose hands had given and given, said: Speak to us of Sacrifice. And he answered, saying: Sacrifice is the seed that falls into the earth and dies, and in dying gives life to the tree. It is not the loss of the self, but the planting of it, that it might bear fruit in another.
