# Subject Registry

A catalog of every subject available in `subjects/`. Each subject is a developed topic — a theme the writer can build a chapter or a whole book around. Each subject's `topic.md` records its **essence**, **facets**, **motifs**, **questions**, and a **sample opening**.

The writer agent reads this registry to discover available subjects, then reads the individual `topic.md` for the full development.

## Subject Details

### 1. `love/` — Love
Eros, agape, philia, storge, and divine love. The flame and the moth, the rose and the thorn, the two who become one.

### 2. `war/` — War
The cause, the battle, the cost, the compassion, the peace. The sword and the shield, the field of the dead, the dove and the olive branch.

### 3. `god/` — God
The creator, the sustainer, the beloved, the mystery, the within. The light and the darkness, the ocean and the drop, the silence that speaks.

### 4. `mother/` — Mother
The giver of life, the nurturer, the teacher, the sacrifice, the memory. The womb and the cradle, the lullaby and the prayer, the earth as mother.

### 5. `nostalgia/` — Nostalgia
The lost place, the lost time, the lost person, the sweetness, the sorrow. The old photograph, the road that leads back, the scent that unlocks memory.

### 6. `political/` — Politics
Power, justice, freedom, the state, the people. The throne and the crowd, the law and the sword, the mask of the ruler.

### 7. `philosophical/` — Philosophy
Metaphysics, ethics, epistemology, logic, the examined life. The cave and the light, the question and the silence, the mirror and the self.

### 8. `death/` — Death
The end, the transition, the grief, the teacher, the mystery. The setting sun, the river to the sea, the closed door and the open gate.

### 9. `nature/` — Nature
The source, the mirror, the teacher, the refuge, the sacred. The river and the sea, the seed and the tree, the storm and the calm.

### 10. `freedom/` — Freedom
Freedom from, freedom to, inner freedom, the cost, the limit. The broken chain, the bird and the sky, the inner citadel.

### 11. `time/` — Time
The past, the present, the future, change, eternity. The river and the sea, the hourglass, the still point and the turning world.

### 12. `childhood/` — Childhood
Wonder, play, innocence, the wound, the memory. The garden and the gate, the first light, the child within the adult.

### 13. `friendship/` — Friendship
The companion, the witness, the equal, the refuge, the test. The two roads that meet, the shared table, the mirror that reflects truly.

### 14. `work/` — Work
The labor, the craft, the service, the meaning, the burden. The seed and the harvest, the hammer and the anvil, the hands that build.

### 15. `joy-sorrow/` — Joy and Sorrow
Joy, sorrow, the unity, the depth, the balance. The sun and the shadow, the cup filled and emptied, the two that are one.

### 16. `beauty/` — Beauty
The visible, the hidden, the awakening, the fleeting, the eternal. The flower and the fruit, the light through the leaf, the form that reveals the formless.

### 17. `knowledge/` — Knowledge
The gathering, the ordering, the power, the limit, the wisdom. The lamp and the darkness, the book and the world, the ocean and the shore.

### 18. `hope/` — Hope
The light, the courage, the trust, the seed, the test. The candle in the dark, the seed in the winter earth, the dawn after the long night.

### 19. `fear/` — Fear
The guardian, the jailer, the unknown, the loss, the courage. The shadow and the light, the cage and the open door, the night and the dawn.

### 20. `justice/` — Justice
The balance, the righting, the law, the mercy, the cry. The scales and the sword, the blindfold and the light, the cry that will not be silenced.

### 21. `solitude/` — Solitude
The silence, the presence, the distinction, the source, the discipline. The empty room, the still water, the well and the spring.

### 22. `sacrifice/` — Sacrifice
The giving, the cause, the cost, the meaning, the memory. The seed that dies to live, the candle that burns to give light, the mother and the child.

## How to Add a New Subject

1. Create a folder `subjects/<name>/`.
2. Write the subject to `subjects/<name>/topic.md`, following the structure: **Essence**, **Facets**, **Motifs**, **Questions**, **Sample Opening**.
3. Add a row to the table above and a detail section below.
