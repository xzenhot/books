# Topic: Nature

## Essence

Nature is the first scripture — the world of river and forest, mountain and sea, in which the truth of things is written without words. It is the source, the mirror, and the teacher of the human soul.

## Facets

- **The source** — the earth from which all life comes.
- **The mirror** — the outer world that reflects the inner.
- **The teacher** — the wisdom of the seed, the river, the season.
- **The refuge** — the peace the wild offers the weary.
- **The sacred** — the divine dwelling in the ordinary.

## Motifs

- The river and the sea
- The seed and the tree
- The mountain and the valley
- The storm and the calm
- The flower and the fruit

## Questions

- What does nature teach us about ourselves?
- Is nature sacred, or merely useful?
- What do we owe the earth that sustains us?
- Can the wild heal the wounds of the city?

## Sample Opening

And a farmer, whose hands knew the soil and whose heart knew the seasons, said: Speak to us of Nature. And he answered, saying: Nature is the first book, written before the first word, and the seed is its first sentence, and the river is its longest prayer.
