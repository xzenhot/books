# Topic: Work

## Essence

Work is the shaping of the world by the hand and the will — the labor by which the seed becomes bread, the stone becomes a house, the thought becomes a thing. It is the way the self is poured into the world, and the world is made to answer the self.

## Facets

- **The labor** — the effort of hand and mind.
- **The craft** — the skill, the mastery, the pride of making.
- **The service** — the work done for others.
- **The meaning** — the purpose work gives to a life.
- **The burden** — the toil that wears, the labor that is not loved.

## Motifs

- The seed and the harvest
- The hammer and the anvil
- The loom and the thread
- The field and the furrow
- The hands that build

## Questions

- Is work a curse or a calling?
- What makes work meaningful?
- Can the laborer love the labor?
- What do we owe the one who works for us?

## Sample Opening

And a plowman, whose hands were hard and whose heart was full, said: Speak to us of Work. And he answered, saying: Work is love made visible. And if you cannot work with love but only with distaste, it is better that you should leave your work and sit at the gate of the temple and take alms of those who work with joy.
