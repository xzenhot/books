# Topic: Beauty

## Essence

Beauty is the radiance of the true — the light that shines through the form of things and awakens the soul to wonder. It is not in the object alone, nor in the eye alone, but in the meeting of the two, where the world reveals its hidden order.

## Facets

- **The visible** — the form, the color, the line.
- **The hidden** — the order, the truth, the meaning beneath.
- **The awakening** — the wonder beauty stirs in the soul.
- **The fleeting** — the beauty that passes, and is precious for passing.
- **The eternal** — the beauty that does not fade.

## Motifs

- The flower and the fruit
- The light through the leaf
- The face and the soul
- The dawn and the dusk
- The form that reveals the formless

## Questions

- Is beauty in the eye, or in the thing?
- Is beauty a luxury or a necessity?
- Why does the fleeting move us more than the lasting?
- Is beauty the same as truth?

## Sample Opening

And a poet, whose eyes were full of the world's light, said: Speak to us of Beauty. And he answered, saying: Beauty is not a need but an ecstasy. It is not a mouth thirsting nor an empty hand stretched forth, but rather a heart enflamed and a soul enchanted.
