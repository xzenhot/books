# Topic: Time

## Essence

Time is the river in which all things flow — the measure of change, the medium of memory and hope, the force that carries the seed to the tree and the tree to the earth. It is the great mystery: the past that is gone, the future that is not yet, and the present that is all we ever hold.

## Facets

- **The past** — memory, the weight of what has been.
- **The present** — the only moment that is real.
- **The future** — hope, fear, the not-yet.
- **Change** — the constant becoming of all things.
- **Eternity** — the timeless, the unchanging beyond time.

## Motifs

- The river and the sea
- The hourglass and the clock
- The seed and the harvest
- The rising and setting sun
- The still point, the turning world

## Questions

- Is the present all we have, or all we are?
- Does the past exist, or only our memory of it?
- Can we hold time, or only be carried by it?
- What is the relationship between time and eternity?

## Sample Opening

And a philosopher, who had watched the river for many years, said: Speak to us of Time. And he answered, saying: You cannot step into the same river twice, for the river is not the same, and neither are you. Yet the river is still the river, and you are still you, and in that stillness time and eternity meet.
