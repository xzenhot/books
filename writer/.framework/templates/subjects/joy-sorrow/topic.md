# Topic: Joy and Sorrow

## Essence

Joy and sorrow are the two faces of one heart — the light and the shadow that fall from the same sun. They are not opposites but companions, each giving the other its depth, and the soul that has not known sorrow cannot know the fullness of joy.

## Facets

- **Joy** — the rising of the heart, the fullness of being.
- **Sorrow** — the falling, the emptying, the grief.
- **The unity** — how each contains and deepens the other.
- **The depth** — the soul carved deeper by sorrow, filled fuller by joy.
- **The balance** — the rhythm of the two in a life.

## Motifs

- The sun and the shadow
- The cup that is filled and emptied
- The river and the drought
- The laughter and the tears
- The two that are one

## Questions

- Can joy exist without sorrow?
- Is sorrow a wound or a deepening?
- How do we hold both at once?
- What does sorrow teach that joy cannot?

## Sample Opening

And a woman, whose heart had known both the heights and the depths, said: Speak to us of Joy and Sorrow. And he answered, saying: Your joy is your sorrow unmasked. And the selfsame well from which your laughter rises was oftentimes filled with your tears.
