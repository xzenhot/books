# Topic: Friendship

## Essence

Friendship is the bond of equals — the love that is chosen, not given by blood or fate. It is the companion on the road, the witness to the self, the one who knows us and stays. Of all loves, it is the freest, for it is given without obligation and kept without chains.

## Facets

- **The companion** — the one who walks beside us.
- **The witness** — the one who sees us as we are.
- **The equal** — the bond without hierarchy.
- **The refuge** — the shelter in the storm.
- **The test** — the friendship that survives time and trial.

## Motifs

- The two roads that meet
- The shared table, the shared bread
- The mirror that reflects truly
- The hand in the dark
- The tree that shelters two

## Questions

- What makes a friend different from a companion?
- Can friendship survive distance and time?
- Is friendship a lesser love, or a purer one?
- What do we owe the one who knows us best?

## Sample Opening

And a young man, whose heart was full of gratitude for one who had stood beside him, said: Speak to us of Friendship. And he answered, saying: Your friend is your needs answered. He is your field which you sow with love and reap with thanksgiving.
