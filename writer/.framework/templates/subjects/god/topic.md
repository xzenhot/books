# Topic: God

## Essence

God is the name we give to the infinite — the source, the ground, the unseen order beneath all things. Whether conceived as a person, a force, or the silence at the heart of being, God is the answer to the question the soul cannot stop asking: why is there something rather than nothing?

## Facets

- **The creator** — the maker of the world, the first cause.
- **The sustainer** — the presence that holds all things in being.
- **The beloved** — the object of the soul's longing, the divine lover.
- **The mystery** — the unknowable, the silence beyond all names.
- **The within** — the divine dwelling in the depths of the self.

## Motifs

- The light and the darkness
- The still small voice
- The ocean and the drop
- The temple and the heart
- The silence that speaks

## Questions

- Can God be known, or only sought?
- Is God within us, beyond us, or both?
- Why does the innocent suffer, if God is good?
- Is the search for God the same as the search for the self?

## Sample Opening

And a seeker, whose soul was restless and whose questions were many, said: Speak to us of God. And he answered, saying: You ask of the ocean while standing in the rain. The drop that falls upon your hand is not the whole of the sea, yet it is not other than the sea.
