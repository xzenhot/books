# Topic: Fear

## Essence

Fear is the shadow that walks before us — the anticipation of harm, the dread of the unknown, the tightening of the heart before the thing that threatens. It is the oldest guardian and the oldest jailer, for it protects us from danger and imprisons us from life.

## Facets

- **The guardian** — the fear that warns of real danger.
- **The jailer** — the fear that binds us to the small and the safe.
- **The unknown** — the dread of what we cannot see.
- **The loss** — the fear of losing what we love.
- **The courage** — the facing of fear, the walking through it.

## Motifs

- The shadow and the light
- The cage and the open door
- The storm and the shelter
- The cliff and the leap
- The night and the dawn

## Questions

- Is fear a weakness or a wisdom?
- What is the difference between caution and cowardice?
- Can we be brave without being afraid?
- What does fear protect, and what does it cost?

## Sample Opening

And a soldier, whose hands had trembled and whose heart had held, said: Speak to us of Fear. And he answered, saying: Fear is the shadow that walks before you, and courage is not the absence of the shadow, but the walking on while it follows. The brave are not those who do not fear, but those who fear and still go forward.
