# Topic: Love

## Essence

Love is the force that draws the separate toward the whole — the longing of one soul for another, of the drop for the ocean, of the self for what completes it. It is at once the most personal and the most universal of human experiences.

## Facets

- **Eros** — the passion of the body, the fire of desire.
- **Agape** — the selfless love that asks nothing in return.
- **Philia** — the love of friendship, the bond of equals.
- **Storge** — the love of family, the bond of blood and habit.
- **Divine love** — the longing of the soul for the Beloved, the infinite.

## Motifs

- The flame and the moth
- The rose and the thorn
- The river and the sea
- The wound and the healing
- The two who become one

## Questions

- What is the difference between loving and being loved?
- Can love survive the loss of its object?
- Is love a gift or a discipline?
- Does love complete the self, or dissolve it?

## Sample Opening

And a young woman, whose heart was full and whose eyes were bright, said: Speak to us of Love. And he answered, saying: When love beckons to you, follow him, though his ways are hard and steep.
