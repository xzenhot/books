# Topic: Philosophy

## Essence

Philosophy is the love of wisdom — the disciplined wonder that asks the deepest questions and refuses the easy answer. It is the mind's journey toward the truth of things: what is real, what is good, what can be known, and how we should live.

## Facets

- **Metaphysics** — what is real, what is the nature of being.
- **Ethics** — what is good, how we should live.
- **Epistemology** — what can be known, and how.
- **Logic** — the structure of sound thought.
- **The examined life** — the self turned inward, questioning.

## Motifs

- The cave and the light
- The question and the silence
- The river and the unchanging
- The seed and the tree
- The mirror and the self

## Questions

- What is the good life?
- Can truth be known, or only sought?
- What is the self, and does it endure?
- Why is there something rather than nothing?

## Sample Opening

And a student, whose mind was restless and whose heart was hungry, said: Speak to us of Philosophy. And he answered, saying: Philosophy is the love that the mind bears for the truth, and the truth is a light that grows brighter the nearer you draw, and never blinds you, but only shows you more.
