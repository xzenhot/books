# Topic: Justice

## Essence

Justice is the balance of the scales — the giving to each what is due, the righting of the wrong, the ordering of the common life so that no one is crushed and no one is exalted beyond their due. It is the oldest demand of the human heart, and the hardest to fulfill.

## Facets

- **The balance** — the scales, the measure, the due.
- **The righting** — the correction of the wrong.
- **The law** — the rule that orders the common life.
- **The mercy** — the justice tempered by compassion.
- **The cry** — the demand of the wronged for redress.

## Motifs

- The scales and the sword
- The blindfold and the light
- The debt and the payment
- The broken and the mended
- The cry that will not be silenced

## Questions

- What is due to each, and who decides?
- Is justice the same as the law?
- Can mercy and justice be reconciled?
- What do we owe the wronged, and the wrongdoer?

## Sample Opening

And a judge, whose scales had weighed many lives, said: Speak to us of Justice. And he answered, saying: Justice is the balance, but the balance is held by a hand, and the hand must be steady and the heart must be true, for the scales that weigh the world are moved by the soul that holds them.
