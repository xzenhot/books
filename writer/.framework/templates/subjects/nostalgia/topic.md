# Topic: Nostalgia

## Essence

Nostalgia is the ache of the past — the longing for a time, a place, a person that is gone and cannot return. It is memory made sweet and sorrowful at once, the heart's way of holding what it has lost.

## Facets

- **The lost place** — the home, the village, the childhood room.
- **The lost time** — the golden age, the season that will not come again.
- **The lost person** — the face, the voice, the love that time took.
- **The sweetness** — the beauty of what was, remembered.
- **The sorrow** — the knowledge that it cannot return.

## Motifs

- The old photograph, the faded letter
- The road that leads back, the door that will not open
- The scent that unlocks memory
- The empty house, the overgrown garden
- The song that was sung once

## Questions

- Is nostalgia a comfort or a wound?
- Do we long for the past, or for a past that never was?
- Can memory be trusted, or does it beautify what it keeps?
- What is the difference between remembering and clinging?

## Sample Opening

And a traveler, far from home, whose heart was full of a place he could not return to, said: Speak to us of Nostalgia. And he answered, saying: Nostalgia is the shadow of a door that has closed behind you, and the light that still falls through its keyhole.
