## Quality Metrics Analysis

### 1. Wit and Wordplay
**Rating: 9.5/10**

Comedy lives in the turn of phrase — paradox, pun, epigram — where language itself becomes the source of delight.

### 2. The Absurd Made Logical
**Rating: 9/10**

Comic structure takes a ridiculous premise and pursues it with perfect, deadpan consistency, so the absurd becomes inevitable.

### 3. Social Satire
**Rating: 9/10**

Comedy holds manners and pretension to the light, exposing folly through laughter rather than lecture.

### 4. Mistaken Identity and Misunderstanding
**Rating: 8.5/10**

The engine of farce is confusion — who is who, who said what — resolved through recognition.

### 5. Cultural Resonance
**Rating: 9/10**

It offers the relief of laughter and the truth of the mirror, both at once.
