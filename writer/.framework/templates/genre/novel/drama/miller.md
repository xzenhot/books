## Document Overview
**Source:** miller.txt
**Work:** Death of a Salesman (1949)
**Author:** Arthur Miller
**Genre:** Drama
**Structure:** Three-part tragedy of a man, his family, and the failure of a dream

---

## Quality Metrics Analysis

### 1. Conflict and Confrontation
**Rating: 9.5/10**

Drama lives in collision: between people, between self and self, between dream and reality. Tension is the substance.

### 2. Character as Action
**Rating: 9/10**

In drama, character is revealed through what is said and done — speech and gesture are the whole medium.

### 3. The Weight of the Past
**Rating: 9/10**

The past presses into the present; memory and consequence drive the present conflict.

### 4. Speech as Revelation
**Rating: 9/10**

Dialogue and monologue carry the burden of meaning; the spoken word is the instrument of truth and self-deception.

### 5. Cultural Resonance
**Rating: 9.5/10**

It stages the common tragedy — the dream that fails, the family that cannot hold — and holds a mirror to the audience.
