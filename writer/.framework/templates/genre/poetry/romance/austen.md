# Book Analysis: Pride and Prejudice

## Document Overview
**Source:** austen.txt
**Work:** Pride and Prejudice (1813)
**Author:** Jane Austen
**Genre:** Romance
**Structure:** Courtship plot of misjudgment, correction, and union

---

## Quality Metrics Analysis

### 1. Courtship Architecture
**Rating: 9.5/10**

Romance is built on the tension between desire and obstacle: first impressions, social barriers, pride, and prejudice. The delay is the engine.

### 2. Character Revelation
**Rating: 9/10**

Love is a form of knowledge. The genre traces how two people come to see each other truly, beyond first error.

### 3. Wit and Dialogue
**Rating: 9/10**

The romantic voice lives in banter, irony, and the charged exchange — attraction conducted through speech.

### 4. Social Constraint as Fuel
**Rating: 8.5/10**

Class, decorum, and reputation are the walls against which feeling presses, giving the union its hard-won meaning.

### 5. Emotional Payoff
**Rating: 9.5/10**

The genre's promise is the earned resolution: misunderstanding yields to understanding, isolation to union.

## Metaphorical Landscape

### Primary Conceptual Framework
- **The Dance:** Courtship as patterned approach and retreat
- **The Estate:** Social standing as landscape to be crossed

- **The Union:** Two selves resolved into one
