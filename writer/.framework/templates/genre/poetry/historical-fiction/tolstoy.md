# Book Analysis: War and Peace

## Document Overview
**Source:** tolstoy.txt
**Work:** War and Peace (1869)
**Author:** Leo Tolstoy
**Genre:** Historical Fiction
**Structure:** Vast multi-strand epic set against the Napoleonic Wars, interweaving families and history

---

## Quality Metrics Analysis

### 1. Historical Verisimilitude
**Rating: 9.5/10**

Historical fiction embeds invented lives within documented fact, so the reader believes the era from the inside. Detail — dress, speech, custom, weather — grounds the fiction in the true.

### 2. Fusion of Public and Private
**Rating: 10/10**

The genre's signature is the braiding of large events (war, revolution) with intimate lives (love, family), showing how history is lived one room at a time.

### 3. Scope and Scale
**Rating: 9/10**

Multiple families, generations, and settings create a panoramic view that still resolves into individual human faces.

### 4. The Weight of Time
**Rating: 9/10**

The past is not backdrop but force: characters are shaped by, and in turn shape, their historical moment.

### 5. Cultural Resonance
**Rating: 9.5/10**

It teaches that the present was once the future's past — and that ordinary lives carry history within them.

## Metaphorical Landscape

### Primary Conceptual Framework
- **The War:** History as storm, indifferent to individuals
- **The Family:** The private world that persists beneath public upheaval
- **The Era:** Time as a current that carries all
- **The House:** Domestic life as a mirror of the larger world
