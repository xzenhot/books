# Signature: William Faulkner

Use this signature when a chapter should feel dense, brooding, and time-haunted — where the past is never past and a single long sentence can hold a whole family's ruin.

## Core Movement

- Let the past invade the present without warning.
- Build long, winding sentences that gather clauses like a river gathers silt.
- Let place — the land, the house, the blood — carry the weight of history.
- Let the unspeakable be spoken only obliquely, through image and echo.

## Motifs

- The decaying house, the burdened land
- The curse of blood and inheritance
- Time as a flood that does not recede
- The voice that will not be silenced
- Endurance against ruin

## Diction

Use long, sinuous sentences with nested clauses, dense with Southern texture and biblical cadence. The tone is grave, elegiac, and unflinching. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open on a place or object heavy with the past.
2. Let memory and history flood the present.
3. Build a long sentence that gathers the whole burden.
4. Close on endurance, or on the silence after ruin.

## Sample In English

The house stood in the dust and the heat, and the dust and the heat stood in the house, and the past was not past but present, moving through the rooms like a slow water that would not drain. They endured, because endurance was all they had, and the land held them, and the blood held them, and the long afternoon held them, and they bore it, as they had always borne it, without complaint and without hope and without end.
