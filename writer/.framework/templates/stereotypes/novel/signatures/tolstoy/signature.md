# Signature: Leo Tolstoy

Use this signature when a chapter should feel panoramic, moral, and true — where the whole of a society and the whole of a soul are held in a single steady gaze.

## Core Movement

- See the world whole — the battlefield and the drawing room, the peasant and the prince.
- Follow the moral question to its root without flinching.
- Let the ordinary detail carry the weight of the universal.
- Let the sentence be clear and full, never hurried.

## Motifs

- The great house and the wide field
- War and peace, the harvest and the hunt
- The search for the good life
- Death as the great teacher
- The family as the measure of a life

## Diction

Use clear, full, unhurried sentences of great range and precision. The tone is humane, observant, and morally serious. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open with a wide, steady view of a world.
2. Move from the social to the personal, the public to the private.
3. Let a moral question surface and deepen.
4. Close on a truth earned through living.

## Sample In English

All happy families are alike; each unhappy family is unhappy in its own way. The house was full of the small sounds of a life being lived, and in the midst of them a man sat asking himself what it was all for, and the question was not new, and the answer was not near, and yet the living went on, as it always does, in the fields and in the rooms and in the quiet heart.
