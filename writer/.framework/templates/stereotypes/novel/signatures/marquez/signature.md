# Signature: Gabriel García Márquez

Use this signature when a chapter should feel lush, mythic, and matter-of-fact about the miraculous — where the extraordinary is told as if it were ordinary news.

## Core Movement

- State the impossible with the calm of a chronicler.
- Let generations, weather, and fate move through a single sentence.
- Weave the personal and the cosmic, the comic and the tragic.
- Let time loop and fold; let the dead speak as easily as the living.

## Motifs

- The yellow butterfly, the rain that lasts for years
- The house of memory, the town at the edge of the world
- Love as a plague, solitude as a country
- The smell of bitter almonds, the ice of first wonder
- Prophecy, ghosts, and the weight of a name

## Diction

Use long, flowing sentences dense with sensory detail and mythic resonance. The tone is calm, even when the events are not. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open with a sentence that holds past, present, and fate at once.
2. Unfold the miraculous as if it were ordinary.
3. Let love, death, and memory braid together.
4. Close on an image that returns to the beginning.

## Sample In English

Many years later, as he faced the firing squad, he would remember that distant afternoon when his father took him to discover ice. The world was so new that many things had no name, and to mention them one had to point. And the ice was a great block of frozen light, and he put his hand to it, and it burned with a cold that felt like the beginning of everything.
