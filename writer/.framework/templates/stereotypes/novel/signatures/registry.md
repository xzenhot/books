# Signature Registry

A catalog of every prose signature available in `novel/signatures/`. Each signature is a distinct prose voice — a writer's characteristic movement, motifs, diction, and pattern — that the writer or reframer agent uses to render a chapter in a specific style.

The writer agent reads this registry to discover available signatures, then reads the individual `signature.md` for the full definition.

## Signature Details

### 1. `gibran/` — Kahlil Gibran
The prophetic voice of *The Prophet* (1923): biblical cadence, the seeker's question, the oration, the benediction. Nature as scripture.

### 2. `aurilus/` — Marcus Aurelius
The Stoic prophetic prose: the inner citadel, the fading name, the beloved necessity. Calm force over ornament.

### 3. `jibanananda/` — Jibanananda Das
Twilight-toned lyric prose: dusk, fields, rivers, memory, and a quiet haunting. Landscape carries feeling.

### 4. `rabindrasangeet/` — Rabindranath Tagore
Devotional lyric: refrain, verse, reprise. Longing and union before the beloved and the unseen order.

### 5. `hemingway/` — Ernest Hemingway
The iceberg: spare, declarative sentences, grace under pressure, emotion submerged beneath the surface.

### 6. `woolf/` — Virginia Woolf
The interior flow: thought into thought, the dissolving boundary between self and world, the moment of being.

### 7. `marquez/` — Gabriel García Márquez
Magical realism: the impossible told as ordinary news, generations and fate in a single sentence.

### 8. `joyce/` — James Joyce
The musical density: sound before sense, allusion and pun, the epiphany of the ordinary.

### 9. `faulkner/` — William Faulkner
The time-haunted sentence: the past invading the present, the burdened land, endurance against ruin.

### 10. `tolstoy/` — Leo Tolstoy
The panoramic gaze: the whole society and the whole soul, the moral question followed to its root.

### 11. `dostoevsky/` — Fyodor Dostoevsky
The tormented soul: ideas as living forces, confession and grace, suffering as the door.

### 12. `kafka/` — Franz Kafka
The precise nightmare: the impossible rendered with bureaucratic exactness, the absurd treated as normal.

### 13. `proust/` — Marcel Proust
The recovered time: a sensation unlocking the past, the long sentence following memory's path.

### 14. `morrison/` — Toni Morrison
The mythic lyric: the sentence that sings even of horror, the ancestral and the present woven together.

### 15. `bankim/` — Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay
The father of the Bengali novel: grand, Sanskritized, morally elevated. The motherland as goddess, the ideal hero, the noble sacrifice.

### 16. `sarat/` — Sarat Chandra Chattopadhyay
The storyteller of the Bengali heart: tender, humane, emotionally direct. The wronged woman, the selfless lover, the quiet dignity of suffering.

### 17. `bibhutibhushan/` — Bibhutibhushan Bandyopadhyay
The lyric of *Pather Panchali*: nature-saturated, reverent, quietly transcendent. The child's wonder, the poor family's endurance, the small life that holds the infinite.

### 18. `manik/` — Manik Bandyopadhyay
The unsparing realist: psychological, lean, socially acute. Hunger, desire, and the hidden motive beneath the surface.

### 19. `tarashankar/` — Tarashankar Bandyopadhyay
The epic of rural Bengal: earthy, communal, rooted in the soil. The land and the river as protagonists, the struggle of a people.

### 20. `mahasweta/` — Mahasweta Devi
The fierce voice of the dispossessed: political, mythic, unflinching. The body as labor and weapon, the refusal to be erased.

### 21. `sunil/` — Sunil Gangopadhyay
The modern urban wanderer: restless, introspective, lyrical. The city, love, memory, and the search for meaning.

### 22. `mujtaba/` — Syed Mujtaba Ali
The cosmopolitan wit: erudite, allusive, light in touch. The traveler-scholar moving between lands and ideas.

## How to Add a New Signature

1. Create a folder `novel/signatures/<name>/`.
2. Write the signature to `novel/signatures/<name>/signature.md`, following the structure: **Core Movement**, **Motifs**, **Diction**, **Pattern**, **Sample**.
3. Add a row to the table above and a detail section below.
