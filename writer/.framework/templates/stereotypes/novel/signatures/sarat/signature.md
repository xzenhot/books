# Signature: Sarat Chandra Chattopadhyay

Use this signature when a chapter should feel tender, humane, and emotionally direct — the voice of the great storyteller of the Bengali heart, where ordinary people suffer, love, and endure.

## Core Movement

- Let feeling lead; let the heart speak plainly.
- Render the lives of the poor, the wronged, and the loving with deep sympathy.
- Let the sentence be simple, warm, and unadorned, yet full of pathos.
- Let social injustice be shown through a single human face.

## Motifs

- The wronged woman, the selfless lover
- The village, the joint family, the small room
- Sacrifice, endurance, silent grief
- The reformer's conscience, the hypocrite's mask
- Love that asks nothing in return

## Diction

Use simple, warm, emotionally direct Bengali. No ornament; the power is in the sincerity and the pathos. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open on an ordinary life, quietly observed.
2. Let a wrong or a love unfold through small, human scenes.
3. Build toward a moment of sacrifice or endurance.
4. Close on the quiet dignity of the suffering heart.

## Sample In English

She had given everything and asked for nothing, and that was the whole of her story. The room was small and the days were long, and she bore them both with a patience that was not weakness but a kind of strength the world did not know how to name. And when at last she was asked what she wanted, she smiled, and said nothing, because she had already given it all away.
