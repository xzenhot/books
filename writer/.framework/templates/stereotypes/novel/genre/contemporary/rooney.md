# Book Analysis: Normal People

## Document Overview
**Source:** rooney.txt
**Work:** Normal People (2018)
**Author:** Sally Rooney
**Genre:** Contemporary
**Structure:** Episodic chapters spanning years, tracing an on-and-off relationship

---

## Quality Metrics Analysis

### 1. Psychological Realism
**Rating: 9/10**

Contemporary fiction renders the interior life of ordinary people with unsparing precision. Thought, hesitation, and miscommunication are the real events.

### 2. Relational Focus
**Rating: 9.5/10**

The central subject is connection: how two people fail and succeed to reach each other across class, time, and self-doubt.

### 3. Voice and Register
**Rating: 8.5/10**

The voice is flat, precise, and modern — spare sentences that carry enormous emotional weight through what is left unsaid.

### 4. Class and Social Texture
**Rating: 8.5/10**

The contemporary novel maps the invisible structures — money, education, family — that shape private feeling.

### 5. Cultural Resonance
**Rating: 9/10**

It speaks to a generation navigating intimacy, identity, and the difficulty of being known.
