# Chapter 001: Gravity

And a seeker said, "Speak to us of gravity."

And he answered, saying:

Gravity is the quiet longing by which every body remembers every other body. It is not a chain, though it binds; it is not a hand, though it draws; it is the patient law by which the scattered stones of the world confess that they are kin.

So too does the soul feel its hidden weight. You are pulled toward home, toward duty, toward the face you have wronged and the work you have left unfinished. Call it burden if you must, but the wise call it relation.

For nothing falls alone. Even the falling leaf is answering the whole earth.
