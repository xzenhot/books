# Chapter 002: Entropy

And a keeper of lamps said, "Speak to us of entropy."

And he answered, saying:

Entropy is the loosening of the knot, the cooling of the ember, the room returning to dust after the feast has ended. Do not hate it, for it is also the mercy by which rigid things are softened and proud towers learn the language of earth.

Order is a guest, not an owner. You may sweep the room, stack the vessels, mend the gate, and sing while doing so; yet the dust will come again, not as enemy but as reminder.

Therefore build with tenderness, for all forms are temporary shelters for the formless.
