# Chapter 005: Equilibrium

And a merchant weighing grain said, "Speak to us of equilibrium."

And he answered, saying:

Equilibrium is not lifeless stillness. It is the living poise of the dancer, the branch that bends and returns, the cup held level while the road is uneven. Balance is not escape from motion; it is wisdom inside motion.

When desire pulls one way and fear another, do not cut the ropes in anger. Stand where the deeper law can be felt. Let the heart become a scale that does not lie.

For the centered soul is not unmoved; it is moved by what is worthy.
