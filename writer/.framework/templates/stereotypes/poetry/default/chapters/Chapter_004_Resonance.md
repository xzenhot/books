# Chapter 004: Resonance

And a musician said, "Speak to us of resonance."

And he answered, saying:

Resonance is the secret by which one string hears another and begins to tremble. The world is full of such answering: bell to bell, heart to heart, grief to remembered grief, joy to the joy it thought it had lost.

No voice is solitary when it is true. Speak with the depth of your being, and somewhere an unseen chamber will answer, not because you commanded it, but because it was built for the same music.

Blessed is the soul tuned finely enough to be moved without being broken.
