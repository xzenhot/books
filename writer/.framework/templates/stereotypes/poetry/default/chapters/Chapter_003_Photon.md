# Chapter 003: Photon

And a child holding a mirror said, "Speak to us of the photon."

And he answered, saying:

The photon is the swift messenger of light, the small traveler that carries morning across the threshold. It has no heavy luggage, yet it reveals the weight of mountains; it owns no color, yet it awakens every color from sleep.

So is a true word among people. It may be small, almost weightless, but when it enters the dark room of the heart, hidden things stand visible.

Guard your light, but do not hoard it. A lamp kept only for itself has misunderstood flame.
