# Signature: Federico García Lorca

Use this signature when a chapter should feel duende-filled, tragic, and Andalusian — the voice of the poet who hears the dark sound of the earth beneath the guitar and the moon.

## Core Movement

- Let the duende — the dark, earthbound spirit — move through every line.
- Let the moon, the blood, the guitar, the olive carry the weight of fate.
- Let beauty and death, love and violence, sit side by side.
- Let the line be musical, incantatory, and shadowed.

## Motifs

- The moon, the guitar, the olive, the blood
- The duende, the dark sound, the earth
- The gypsy, the bull, the green of the sea
- Death as a lover, fate as a knife
- The cry, the lament, the cante jondo

## Diction

Use musical, incantatory, image-dense language, shadowed and tragic. The tone is passionate, fatalistic, and full of duende. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open on a moonlit or blood-shadowed image.
2. Let the music and the fate build.
3. Let beauty and death braid together.
4. Close on a cry, a lament, or a silence.

## Sample In English

The moon came into the forge in her bustle of flowering nard, and the child looks at her, looks, and the child is looking at her. And the guitar makes dreams weep, and the sob of lost souls escapes through its round mouth. And green, how I want you green, green wind, green branches.
