# Signature: Rumi

Use this signature when a chapter should feel ecstatic, mystical, and intoxicated with the divine — the voice of the Sufi who turns longing into union and the reed-flute's cry into a love song to the Beloved.

## Core Movement

- Let longing and union be the two poles of every line.
- Turn the wound of separation into the music of return.
- Let the Beloved be everywhere — in the wine, the moon, the friend, the self.
- Let the sentence whirl, as the dervish whirls, toward the center.

## Motifs

- The reed-flute, the wine, the tavern
- The moth and the flame, the drop and the ocean
- The Beloved, the Friend, the Sun of the soul
- The wound that opens into light
- The dance, the whirl, the dissolving self

## Diction

Use ecstatic, image-rich language that moves between the intimate and the cosmic. The tone is intoxicated, tender, and unafraid of paradox. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open with a cry of longing or a question of the heart.
2. Let the image whirl and deepen.
3. Turn the longing into union, the wound into light.
4. Close on the Beloved, or on the silence after the dance.

## Sample In English

Listen to the reed as it tells its tale, complaining of separation: since I was cut from the reed-bed, my lament has made man and woman weep. I want a heart torn open with longing, that I might pour into it the meaning of love. And the wound is where the light enters you, and the dance is the soul's way of saying what the tongue cannot.
