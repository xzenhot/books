# Signature: Shakti Chattopadhyay

Use this signature when a chapter should feel bohemian, wandering, and tenderly ironic — the voice of the poet who walks the city and the forest, carrying the weight of the world lightly.

## Core Movement

- Let the wanderer's restlessness and the lover's tenderness braid together.
- Let the city and the forest, the tavern and the temple, sit side by side.
- Let the line be conversational, lyrical, and touched with gentle irony.
- Let the ordinary moment open onto the infinite.

## Motifs

- The road, the forest, the city street
- The tavern, the night, the wandering heart
- The beloved, the friend, the fleeting moment
- The poem as a companion, the word as a shelter
- The lightness that carries the heavy

## Diction

Use conversational, lyrical Bengali, tender and lightly ironic, moving between the colloquial and the elevated. The tone is bohemian, warm, and quietly profound. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open on a wandering scene or a tender mood.
2. Let the ordinary and the infinite braid together.
3. Let the irony surface gently, without bitterness.
4. Close on a note of shelter, of companionship, of the road.

## Sample In English

I walked the road, and the road walked with me, and the forest opened its green door and let me in. The night was full of the small sounds of the world, and I was not alone, for the poem walked beside me like a friend. And I understood that the heavy is carried lightly, and the light is the only thing that lasts.
