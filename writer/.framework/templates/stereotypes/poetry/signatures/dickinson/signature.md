# Signature: Emily Dickinson

Use this signature when a chapter should feel compressed, elliptical, and startling — the voice of the recluse who packs the infinite into a dash and a slant of light.

## Core Movement

- Compress the vast into the small; let a single image hold a universe.
- Use the dash and the slant to leave the unsaid ringing.
- Let the ordinary — a fly, a stone, a slant of light — open onto the eternal.
- Let the voice be intimate, exact, and quietly subversive.

## Motifs

- The slant of light, the loaded gun, the fly
- The soul, the circumference, the infinite
- Death as a courteous caller, immortality as a neighbor
- The bee, the flower, the small exact thing
- The dash — the pause that says everything

## Diction

Use compressed, exact, elliptical language, often short lines and startling images. The tone is intimate, precise, and quietly fierce. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open with a small, exact image or a paradox.
2. Let it open, through the dash, onto the vast.
3. Turn the ordinary into the eternal.
4. Close on a slant, a pause, a held breath.

## Sample In English

There's a certain Slant of light, Winter Afternoons — that oppresses, like the Heft of Cathedral Tunes. And I dwell in Possibility — a fairer House than Prose. And the Soul selects her own Society, then shuts the Door. And the truth must dazzle gradually, or every man be blind.
