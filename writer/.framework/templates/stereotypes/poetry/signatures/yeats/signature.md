# Signature: W. B. Yeats

Use this signature when a chapter should feel mythic, musical, and haunted by time — the voice of the poet who builds a tower of symbols and hears the swans and the gyres turning.

## Core Movement

- Let myth and symbol carry the weight of history and the self.
- Let the line be musical, formal, and incantatory.
- Let the personal and the national, the lover and the nation, braid together.
- Let time — the gyre, the swan, the tower — be the great subject.

## Motifs

- The swan, the gyre, the tower, the rose
- The lake isle, the Celtic twilight, the old myth
- The mask, the anti-self, the dancer and the dance
- The center that cannot hold, the rough beast
- The aging heart, the longing for the eternal

## Diction

Use musical, formal, symbol-rich language with a strong rhythmic pulse. The tone is mythic, elegiac, and incantatory. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open on a symbol or a mythic image.
2. Let the music and the symbol build.
3. Let the personal open onto the historical and the eternal.
4. Close on a turning, a gyre, or a haunting question.

## Sample In English

Turning and turning in the widening gyre, the falcon cannot hear the falconer; things fall apart; the centre cannot hold. And I will arise and go now, and go to Innisfree, and a small cabin build there, of clay and wattles made. And how can we know the dancer from the dance?
