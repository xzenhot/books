17 November, the year you left.

My dear,

I am writing this on the back of a receipt because I could not find clean paper and I could not wait for the shop to open. The window here looks onto the lane where we used to stand and argue about nothing. The rain is doing what it did then — filling the gutter, forgiving no one.

You asked me once what I would keep if I had to leave. I said nothing, because I was lying. I would keep the way you looked at the door before you went out, as if the door had done something to you.

I have kept it. It sits in me like a splinter.

Forgive the crossed-out words. I wrote your name three times and could not let any of them stand, because seeing it makes the distance real, and I am not ready for it to be real.

Write back. Or don't. But if you read this, know that the rain is still falling, and I am still at the window.

Yours,
— always, even now,
the one who stayed.
