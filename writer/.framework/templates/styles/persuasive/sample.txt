You have been told to wait. You have been told, again and again, that the time is not right, that the change is too costly, that the old way has always worked well enough. I am asking you to stop believing it.

Consider what waiting has cost us already. Every year we delay, the work grows harder and the price grows steeper. The river we could have crossed in a single morning is now a season wide. You know this. You have felt it in your own life, in the small, accumulating failures that felt, each one, too minor to act on — until together they became a wall.

You may say it is too late now. That is the oldest lie, and it is told to comfort the comfortable. It is not too late. It is only late, and late is still a time that can be answered.

The people who tell you nothing can be done are not thinking of you. They are thinking of what they stand to lose if you move. Do not let their caution become your chains.

I am not asking you to be reckless. I am asking you to begin. One step, taken today, is worth a thousand plans made for a tomorrow that never comes. The river is rising. The morning is here.

Cross it. Begin. Now.
