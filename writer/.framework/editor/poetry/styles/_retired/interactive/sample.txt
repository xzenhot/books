You stand at the end of the lane. Behind you, the house you left; before you, the road that splits in two. The left path runs to the river, where the ferryman waits and will not wait long. The right path climbs to the station, where the last train is already sounding its whistle.

Choose.

If you take the river, you will hear the water before you see it, and the ferryman will not ask your name. He will ask only whether you are certain. Are you?

If you take the station, you will run, and the running itself will feel like an answer, though you will not know to what.

Neither path is wrong. Both are yours. But know this: whatever you leave behind will be waiting for you at the end of whichever road you choose, patient as the dark.

So. The whistle blows. The water moves. You are still standing at the fork.

Step.
