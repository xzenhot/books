# Topic: Politics

## Essence

Politics is the art of the common life — the struggle over power, justice, and the ordering of the many into a people. It is the arena where the individual meets the collective, and where the question of how we should live together is fought and answered.

## Facets

- **Power** — who holds it, who seeks it, who is ruled by it.
- **Justice** — the fair ordering of the common life.
- **Freedom** — the space the individual is granted within the whole.
- **The state** — the machinery of rule, the law, the institution.
- **The people** — the many whose fate is decided in the arena.

## Motifs

- The throne and the crowd
- The law and the sword
- The speech and the silence
- The revolution and the reform
- The mask of the ruler, the voice of the ruled

## Questions

- What is the just ordering of a society?
- Is power a trust or a possession?
- Can freedom and order be reconciled?
- What do we owe the stranger, the poor, the future?

## Sample Opening

And a statesman, weary of the arena, whose hands had held both the pen and the sword, said: Speak to us of Politics. And he answered, saying: Politics is the mirror in which a people sees its own face, and the forge in which it shapes the life it will live together.
