# Topic: Death

## Essence

Death is the one certainty, the shadow that walks beside every life. It is the end of the body and the great question of the soul — the door through which all must pass, and the mirror in which the meaning of life is finally seen.

## Facets

- **The end** — the cessation of the body, the last breath.
- **The transition** — the passage, the change, the unknown beyond.
- **The grief** — the loss borne by those who remain.
- **The teacher** — death as the measure of what matters.
- **The mystery** — what, if anything, lies beyond.

## Motifs

- The setting sun, the falling leaf
- The river to the sea, the seed to the earth
- The closed door, the open gate
- The candle and the dawn
- The harvest and the winter

## Questions

- Is death an end or a transformation?
- What does the certainty of death teach us about life?
- How should we grieve, and how should we remember?
- Does the fear of death diminish or deepen life?

## Sample Opening

And an old man, whose days were few and whose eyes were calm, said: Speak to us of Death. And he answered, saying: Death is not the opposite of life, but its completion. The river does not fear the sea; it is the sea, returning to itself.
