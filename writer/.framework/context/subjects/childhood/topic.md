# Topic: Childhood

## Essence

Childhood is the first world — the time of wonder, of play, of the self before it learned to hide. It is the garden from which we are all exiled, and the memory to which the heart returns when it seeks its own beginning.

## Facets

- **Wonder** — the world seen fresh, before habit dulled it.
- **Play** — the freedom of the unburdened self.
- **Innocence** — the self before the knowledge of good and evil.
- **The wound** — the first hurt, the first loss, the first fear.
- **The memory** — the child we carry within, always.

## Motifs

- The garden and the gate
- The toy and the dream
- The first light, the first word
- The lost home, the remembered room
- The child within the adult

## Questions

- What is lost when childhood ends?
- Is innocence a gift or an ignorance?
- Can the adult return to the child's wonder?
- What does the child we were teach the person we are?

## Sample Opening

And a man, grown and weary, whose heart still held the echo of a child's laughter, said: Speak to us of Childhood. And he answered, saying: Childhood is the garden before the gate was closed, and the child is the gardener who did not yet know the name of the fruit, and so tasted it with wonder.
