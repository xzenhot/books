# Prose Sample 1 — Sacrifice

## A Tale of Two Cities

**By Charles Dickens** (England, 1812–1870)

*Source: A Tale of Two Cities (1859). Public domain worldwide.*

---

It is a far, far better thing that I do, than I have ever done; it is a far,
far better rest that I go to than I have ever known.

---

## Notes

- **Writer:** Charles Dickens, the English novelist.
- **Country:** England.
- **Era:** Mid-19th century; the novel was published in 1859.
- **Theme:** Sacrifice as redemption — Sydney Carton, who gives his life in place of another, and in doing so finds, at last, a meaning and a rest he had never known.
- **Contrast:** Where the poems in this subject show sacrifice in the abstract, Dickens's closing words give sacrifice a single, human face. It is the sacrifice that redeems a wasted life.
