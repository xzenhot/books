# Topic: Solitude

## Essence

Solitude is the room of the self — the silence in which the soul hears its own voice, the aloneness that is not loneliness but presence. It is the place where the self is met, and the source from which all true company is drawn.

## Facets

- **The silence** — the quiet in which the self speaks.
- **The presence** — the self met, not fled.
- **The distinction** — solitude from loneliness, presence from absence.
- **The source** — the inner well from which company is drawn.
- **The discipline** — the practice of being alone well.

## Motifs

- The empty room, the still water
- The mountain and the hermit
- The seed in the dark earth
- The well and the spring
- The silence that is full

## Questions

- Is solitude a gift or a burden?
- What is the difference between solitude and loneliness?
- Can we be alone without being lonely?
- What does the self say when the world is silent?

## Sample Opening

And a hermit, whose years had been spent in the quiet, said: Speak to us of Solitude. And he answered, saying: Solitude is not the absence of others, but the presence of the self. The lonely man flees the silence; the solitary man enters it, and finds there the one he had been seeking in the crowd.
