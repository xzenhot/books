# Topic: Knowledge

## Essence

Knowledge is the light of the mind — the gathering of what is true, the ordering of what is known, the power to see the world as it is. It is the fruit of wonder and the seed of wisdom, and it is never complete, for every answer opens a new question.

## Facets

- **The gathering** — the learning, the study, the seeking.
- **The ordering** — the making sense of what is known.
- **The power** — what knowledge enables us to do.
- **The limit** — what cannot be known, the mystery that remains.
- **The wisdom** — knowledge turned toward the good.

## Motifs

- The lamp and the darkness
- The book and the world
- The seed and the tree
- The ladder and the height
- The ocean and the shore

## Questions

- Is knowledge power, or is wisdom?
- Can we know too much and understand too little?
- What is the difference between knowledge and wisdom?
- Is there a knowledge that is dangerous?

## Sample Opening

And a scholar, whose shelves were full and whose heart was hungry still, said: Speak to us of Knowledge. And he answered, saying: Knowledge is the lamp, but wisdom is the light, and the lamp is nothing without the light. Gather the oil, but do not forget to light the flame.
