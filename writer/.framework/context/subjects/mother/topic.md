# Topic: Mother

## Essence

The mother is the first world — the body from which we come, the voice that first names us, the love that asks nothing and gives everything. She is the origin, the refuge, and the measure of all later love.

## Facets

- **The giver of life** — the body that bore us, the first home.
- **The nurturer** — the hands that fed, the arms that held.
- **The teacher** — the first words, the first values, the first love.
- **The sacrifice** — the self given away, the life poured out.
- **The memory** — the mother we carry within, long after she is gone.

## Motifs

- The womb and the cradle
- The breast and the bread
- The lullaby and the prayer
- The earth as mother, the mother as earth
- The empty chair, the remembered voice

## Questions

- What do we owe the one who gave us life?
- Can a mother's love ever be repaid?
- How does the mother shape the self we become?
- What remains of the mother when she is gone?

## Sample Opening

And a child, grown now, whose heart still turned toward the first voice it had ever known, said: Speak to us of the Mother. And he answered, saying: The mother is the first sky under which you learned to breathe, and the last home to which your heart returns when the world grows cold.
