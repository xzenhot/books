# Topic: Freedom

## Essence

Freedom is the space in which the self becomes itself — the absence of chains, the presence of choice, the power to act and to be. It is the oldest hunger of the human heart, and the hardest to hold.

## Facets

- **Freedom from** — the breaking of chains, the end of bondage.
- **Freedom to** — the power to choose, to act, to become.
- **Inner freedom** — the liberty of the mind no tyrant can touch.
- **The cost** — the responsibility that freedom demands.
- **The limit** — where my freedom meets yours.

## Motifs

- The broken chain, the open cage
- The bird and the sky
- The open road, the open sea
- The inner citadel
- The dawn after the long night

## Questions

- Is freedom the absence of chains, or the presence of choice?
- Can a person be free while others are bound?
- What is the freedom of the mind, and can it be taken?
- Does freedom demand more of us than bondage?

## Sample Opening

And a prisoner, newly released, whose wrists still remembered the weight of the chain, said: Speak to us of Freedom. And he answered, saying: Freedom is not the absence of walls, but the presence of a door, and the courage to walk through it, and the wisdom to know that the truest freedom is the one no wall can contain.
