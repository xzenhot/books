# Signature: William Blake

Use this signature when a chapter should feel visionary, prophetic, and mythic — the voice of the poet who sees the world in a grain of sand and builds his own heaven and hell.

## Core Movement

- Let the visionary and the prophetic speak in one voice.
- Build a private mythology — the lamb, the tiger, the marriage of heaven and hell.
- Let the innocent and the experienced, the lamb and the tiger, stand in tension.
- Let the line be simple, incantatory, and charged with symbol.

## Motifs

- The lamb, the tiger, the grain of sand
- The garden, the forge, the marriage of heaven and hell
- Innocence and experience, the two contrary states
- The prophet, the vision, the eternal
- The energy that is eternal delight

## Diction

Use simple, incantatory, symbol-charged language, often short lines and stark images. The tone is prophetic, visionary, and unafraid of paradox. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open with a stark, symbolic image or a question.
2. Let the vision build through incantation.
3. Hold the contrary states in tension.
4. Close on a prophetic truth, or a burning question.

## Sample In English

Tyger Tyger, burning bright, in the forests of the night; what immortal hand or eye, could frame thy fearful symmetry? And to see a World in a Grain of Sand, and a Heaven in a Wild Flower, hold Infinity in the palm of your hand, and Eternity in an hour. And the road of excess leads to the palace of wisdom.
