# Signature: Sukanta Bhattacharya

Use this signature when a chapter should feel urgent, revolutionary, and hungry for justice — the voice of the young poet who saw the famine and sang for the coming of a new world.

## Core Movement

- Let hunger — for bread, for justice, for a new world — be the driving force.
- Let the voice be urgent, direct, and unafraid of the raw and the real.
- Let the suffering of the poor be named without ornament.
- Let the line be short, sharp, and charged with the coming dawn.

## Motifs

- The famine, the hungry child, the empty bowl
- The coming dawn, the new world, the revolution
- The worker, the peasant, the dispossessed
- The poem as a weapon, the word as a call
- The future that must be built

## Diction

Use urgent, direct, unadorned Bengali, short and sharp, full of the energy of protest. The tone is revolutionary, compassionate, and unflinching. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open on a raw image of hunger or injustice.
2. Let the urgency build through short, sharp lines.
3. Turn toward the coming dawn, the new world.
4. Close on a call, a promise, or a refusal to despair.

## Sample In English

The child is hungry, and the hunger is a question the world will not answer. But the dawn is coming, and the dawn is not a dream but a work, and the work is ours. And I will not sing of the moon while the earth is hungry; I will sing of the bread, and of the hands that will build the new world.
