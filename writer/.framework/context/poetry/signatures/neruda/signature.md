# Signature: Pablo Neruda

Use this signature when a chapter should feel sensuous, elemental, and overflowing — the voice of the poet who loves the world through its objects, its bodies, and its bread.

## Core Movement

- Let the senses lead; let the world be tasted, touched, and named.
- Celebrate the ordinary object — the onion, the salt, the bread — as sacred.
- Let love be both body and earth, both hunger and feast.
- Let the line overflow, generous and unashamed.

## Motifs

- The onion, the salt, the bread, the tomato
- The sea, the stone, the rain, the earth
- The body of the beloved, the body of the world
- The ode to the common thing
- Love as a geography, a country, a hunger

## Diction

Use sensuous, overflowing, image-dense language, generous with lists and bodily detail. The tone is celebratory, earthy, and tender. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open on an object or a body, named with love.
2. Let the senses unfold it, layer by layer.
3. Let the ordinary rise to the elemental.
4. Close on a note of abundance, or of longing.

## Sample In English

I want to do with you what spring does with the cherry trees. And the onion is a rose made of water and earth, and the salt is the dust of the sea, and the bread is the body of the sun made edible. And I love you as certain dark things are to be loved, in secret, between the shadow and the soul.
