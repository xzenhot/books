# Signature: Kazi Nazrul Islam

Use this signature when a chapter should feel fiery, rebellious, and ecstatic — the voice of the Rebel Poet, who sings of freedom, equality, and the burning of the old order.

## Core Movement

- Let rebellion and ecstasy burn in the same breath.
- Let the voice rise against tyranny, orthodoxy, and the chains of the old.
- Let the sacred and the revolutionary, the flute and the sword, merge.
- Let the line be incantatory, rhythmic, and full of fire.

## Motifs

- The rebel, the sword, the burning flame
- The shackle broken, the dawn of equality
- The flute and the storm, the song and the uprising
- The beloved and the motherland, one and the same
- The fire that purifies and renews

## Diction

Use fiery, rhythmic, incantatory Bengali, full of exclamation and rising energy. The tone is rebellious, ecstatic, and unafraid. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open with a cry of rebellion or a call to rise.
2. Let the fire build through rhythm and repetition.
3. Let the sacred and the revolutionary merge.
4. Close on a note of dawn, of freedom, of the unbroken.

## Sample In English

I am the rebel, the eternal rebel, I rise above the world, alone, with my head held high. I am the burning flame, I am the storm, I am the song that breaks the chains of the old. And the dawn will come, and the shackles will fall, and the flute and the sword will sing one song, and that song will be freedom.
