**Work:** The Hunger Games (2008)
**Author:** Suzanne Collins
**Genre:** Young Adult

## Quality Metrics Analysis

### 1. The Coming-of-Age Arc
**Rating: 9.5/10**

Young adult fiction centers the threshold between childhood and adulthood: identity, autonomy, and the first hard choices.

### 2. Immediate, Authentic Voice
**Rating: 9/10**

The genre speaks in a voice the reader recognizes as their own — present, direct, and emotionally true.

### 3. High-Stakes Ordeal
**Rating: 9/10**

The protagonist is tested by danger that forces growth; survival is also self-discovery.

### 4. Moral Awakening
**Rating: 9/10**

The young hero confronts injustice and must decide what kind of person to become.

### 5. Cultural Resonance
**Rating: 9.5/10**

It gives young readers a mirror and a map — a self to recognize and a world to navigate.
