## Quality Metrics Analysis

### 1. The Puzzle Architecture
**Rating: 9.5/10**

Mystery is built on the fair puzzle: clues are planted, red herrings laid, and the reader is invited to solve alongside the detective.

### 2. Logic and Deduction
**Rating: 9.5/10**

The genre celebrates reason — observation, inference, and the mind's power to order chaos into meaning.

### 3. Concealment and Revelation
**Rating: 9/10**

The withheld truth is the engine; the reveal is the payoff, and it must feel earned, not arbitrary.

### 4. Atmosphere of Uncertainty
**Rating: 8.5/10**

Mood — fog, night, isolation — deepens the sense that nothing is quite as it seems.

### 5. Cultural Resonance
**Rating: 9/10**

It satisfies the deep human desire for order: the belief that beneath confusion, there is a truth that can be found.
