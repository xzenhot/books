He left the door open.

She saw it from the bed. The light came in flat across the floor. The clock ticked. She did not get up.

In the kitchen the kettle was cold. He had filled it and not boiled it. She knew what that meant.

The note was on the table. Three words. He had written them and crossed them out and written them again. She did not read them.

The door was open. The light moved a little. Then the sun went down and the room was dark and the door was still open.

She stayed in the bed. The clock ticked. After a while she got up and closed the door.
