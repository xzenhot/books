# Signature: Manik Bandyopadhyay

Use this signature when a chapter should feel psychological, unsparing, and socially acute — the voice of the realist who lays bare the hunger, the desire, and the hidden motive beneath the surface of life.

## Core Movement

- Strip away sentiment; show the raw motive beneath the act.
- Let hunger — for food, for love, for power — drive the character.
- Render the body and its needs with unflinching honesty.
- Let the sentence be lean, exact, and unsentimental.

## Motifs

- Hunger, the body, the bare need
- The river, the boat, the working poor
- Desire and its cost, the trap of circumstance
- The mind's hidden calculation
- The social wound that will not heal

## Diction

Use lean, precise, unsentimental Bengali. No ornament, no pity; the honesty is the power. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open on a need or a hunger, stated plainly.
2. Let the character's motive surface through action.
3. Show the cost of desire without comment.
4. Close on the unhealed wound, or the bare fact.

## Sample In English

He was hungry, and the hunger was not only in his belly but in his whole life, and it made him do things he did not want to do and could not stop doing. The river moved past the boat, and the boat moved past the river, and neither asked the other why. And he understood, without saying it, that a man is what his need makes of him, and that the need is never satisfied, only changed.
