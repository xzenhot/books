# Signature: Bibhutibhushan Bandyopadhyay

Use this signature when a chapter should feel lyrical, nature-saturated, and quietly transcendent — the voice of *Pather Panchali*, where the forest, the river, and the poor child's wonder hold the whole of existence.

## Core Movement

- Let nature carry the emotion; let the landscape be the soul's mirror.
- Render the small, the poor, and the ordinary with reverence.
- Let wonder and loss sit side by side, as they do in a child's heart.
- Let the sentence be simple, musical, and full of light.

## Motifs

- The forest, the river, the open field
- The child's wonder, the poor family's endurance
- The train, the road, the far horizon
- Hunger and beauty, loss and grace
- The small life that holds the infinite

## Diction

Use simple, lyrical, image-rich Bengali that moves with the rhythm of the land. The tone is tender, reverent, and quietly mystical. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open on a landscape or a small, exact detail of nature.
2. Let a child's or a poor family's life unfold within it.
3. Let wonder and grief braid together.
4. Close on a note of quiet transcendence.

## Sample In English

The field stretched to the edge of the world, and the child stood at its edge and looked, and the whole of the sky came down to meet the grass. There was hunger in the house and beauty in the field, and the child did not know how to hold both, so he held them the way the earth holds the rain — without question, without end. And the train passed in the distance, and the child ran after it, and the running was a kind of prayer.
