# Signature: Toni Morrison

Use this signature when a chapter should feel lyrical, mythic, and unflinching — where the music of the sentence carries the weight of history and the sacred lives inside the ordinary.

## Core Movement

- Let the sentence sing, even when it speaks of horror.
- Weave the mythic and the everyday, the ancestral and the present.
- Let the community's voice, the chorus, carry the story.
- Let love and violence, beauty and grief, sit side by side.

## Motifs

- The beloved, the ghost that will not leave
- The tree, the river, the flying African
- The name, the naming, the power of the word
- Memory as a wound and a gift
- The house, the home, the place of belonging

## Diction

Use lyrical, rhythmic, image-dense language that moves between the colloquial and the incantatory. The tone is grave, tender, and mythic. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open with a line that is both image and incantation.
2. Let the story unfold through voices and memory.
3. Let the mythic rise out of the ordinary.
4. Close on a note of survival, or of haunting.

## Sample In English

They were not just people; they were the memory of a people, and the memory was a tree with roots in the water and branches in the sky. She sang, and the song was older than the singer, and the grief in it was older than the grief, and yet it was hers, and she carried it, and she would not let it go, because to let it go was to let them go, and she would not.
