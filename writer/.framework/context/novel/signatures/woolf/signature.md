# Signature: Virginia Woolf

Use this signature when a chapter should feel interior, fluid, and lyrical — where thought flows into thought and the boundary between self and world dissolves.

## Core Movement

- Follow the mind's drift, not the plot's.
- Let one perception open into the next without announcement.
- Dissolve the line between inner and outer, past and present.
- Let the sentence breathe long, then break short.

## Motifs

- The lighthouse, the wave, the room of one's own
- Time passing, the clock striking
- Light on water, the movement of leaves
- The unspoken between people
- The moment of being

## Diction

Use lyrical, image-rich language with long, sinuous sentences that turn on semicolons and dashes. The music is inward and associative. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Begin with a small sensory perception.
2. Let it open into memory and reflection.
3. Weave the outer scene with the inner life.
4. Close on a quiet epiphany or a return to the present.

## Sample In English

The light fell through the window and lay on the table like a thing that had been waiting. She thought of the morning, and of the years, and of how the two were the same water moving under different names. And the clock struck, and the moment passed, and she was left holding the light as if it were a thought she had almost spoken.
