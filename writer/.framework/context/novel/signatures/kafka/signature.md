# Signature: Franz Kafka

Use this signature when a chapter should feel precise, surreal, and quietly terrifying — where the ordinary is rendered with bureaucratic exactness and the absurd is treated as normal.

## Core Movement

- Describe the impossible with the calm precision of a clerk.
- Let the sentence be clean, exact, and unadorned.
- Let the nightmare emerge from the ordinary, not from the fantastic.
- Let the protagonist be reasonable in an unreasonable world.

## Motifs

- The door that will not open, the trial without a charge
- The transformation, the metamorphosis
- The endless corridor, the unreachable authority
- Guilt without a crime, judgment without a judge
- The small, exact detail that betrays the whole

## Diction

Use clean, precise, almost clinical language. No ornament, no exclamation. The horror lives in the exactness. Render recurring terms in the configured target language.

## Pattern

1. Open with a flat, factual statement of the impossible.
2. Follow the protagonist's reasonable attempts to cope.
3. Let the absurd logic tighten around them.
4. Close on a quiet, unresolved image.

## Sample In English

One morning, when he woke from troubled dreams, he found himself changed in his bed into a monstrous insect. He lay on his hard back and saw, as he lifted his head, his brown belly divided into stiff sections. And the first thing he thought was not of the horror of it, but of the train he would miss, and the work that would not wait.
