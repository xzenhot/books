## Subject

The gist grounds the sequence in the painted havelis of Mandawa and Nawalgarh in Shekhawati, Rajasthan, and in the twilight of the Marwari merchant class. The poems also draw on the work of making a wall image: plaster, pigment, brushes, scaffolding, and the conditions of painting at night.

## Research cautions

- Verify the dates, patronage, and conservation history of any specific haveli before including them as fact.
- Do not assume that all Shekhawati murals use the same technique. Confirm whether a particular work is true fresco, a secco, or another mural method before describing its materials.
- The identity and working conditions of individual painters should not be invented. The theme of anonymity is not evidence that every maker was anonymous.

## Remaining to research

- The chronology and regional variation of Shekhawati haveli paintings.
- The materials and methods used for specific murals in Mandawa and Nawalgarh.
- The social and economic conditions of mural painters and their patrons.
