Delhi — born as the legendary Indraprastha, destroyed and rebuilt seven times over three thousand years, and still a seat of power as India's capital today — is remembered here through a chain of small stories tracing the rise and fall of every dynasty that ruled it.

## Expansion

The city of Delhi is the enduring protagonist — older than any empire that has claimed it, and destined to outlive them all. Its conflict is the unbroken cycle of destruction and rebirth: seven times razed, seven times rebuilt, each conqueror certain he had founded the final city, each ruin quietly becoming the foundation of the next. The transformation is the wisdom the city keeps teaching its people — that power is borrowed and glory impermanent — and in the small stories that follow, we watch dynasties rise and fall while the city beneath them endures.
