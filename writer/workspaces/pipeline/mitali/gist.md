# Mitali — The Forty Friends

Forty school friends — twenty boys and twenty girls — reach back across the years to reconnect, because a life is not a life without the talk of the mind, the shared feeling, and the simple touch; together they summon every human emotion and let it surface through the nostalgia of a Bengal village and the small, freely-offered things of childhood.

## Expansion

The speaker is one among them, calling "Friend, where are you?" into the years that scattered forty classmates to the wind. Their conflict is the distance time has made — the silences, the grown-up lives, the unspoken feelings that never found words. Through memory they return to the Bengal village of their school days, where every small thing — a shared tiffin, a monsoon walk, a song, a touch — becomes a doorway to a feeling, until reconnection itself becomes the transformation: the talk of the mind and the touch of the hand made whole again.
