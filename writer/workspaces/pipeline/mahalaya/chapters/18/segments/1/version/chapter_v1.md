# মহালয়া: এর কবিতা

## Workshop

Since the provided epic only contains 15 chapters, Chapter 18 is conceptualized as a concluding, reflective piece that synthesizes the journey of Arunima and Anirban. This chapter explores the lingering resonance of Mahalaya's sacred light after the initial fervor has passed, focusing on the internal struggle to integrate profound spiritual realization into the mundane reality. The central conflict is the tension between transcendent experience and quotidian existence, symbolized by the fading scent of incense against the backdrop of everyday life. The open question remains whether the 'serene unease' achieved during the festival can become the permanent texture of their lives.
