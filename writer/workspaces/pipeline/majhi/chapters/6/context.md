- Juxtaposing the mathematical certainty of the planned engineering schematics against the chaotic, organic measurements of tidal drift and seasonal current.
- Treating the river not as a fluid entity but as an ancient waterway network whose function is being digitally mapped and overridden by modern infrastructure data.

### story_options

- A specific section where a maintenance worker from the construction crew mocks the majhi’s old route, treating it with the same patronizing disdain one might reserve for local wildlife.
- The majhi attempts to navigate a micro-channel or slipstream created between two new concrete pylons; Desire: To maintain established passage; Obstacle: The flow is too unpredictable and restricted by human placement.
- A slow, ritualistic scene where the majhi uses an ancient knot (a navigational sign) that becomes useless because the river's physical geography has been redrawn.

### subject_rotations

- The conflict framed as a collision between two different understandings of 'ownership': natural custodianship versus sovereign development rights.
- Focusing on the auditory displacement: how manufactured noise (engines, pile-drivers) permanently overwrites the sonic identity of the river.
- Treating the bridge/dam not as progress, but as an archaeological blockage—a modern fossil replacing a living habitat.

### sensory_details

- The gritty texture of dried silt carried on the undercurrent against the oiled wood of the hull.
- The sharp metallic tang in the air near heavy construction machinery, contrasting with the earthy smell of exposed river mud.
- The way sunlight fractures and dies upon hitting a massive, uniform expanse of grey concrete.
