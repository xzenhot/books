## chapter_summary

Chapter 28, "পা-চাটা," delves into the corrosive nature of sycophancy within the political landscape, vividly portraying the act of flattery and subservience—the 'পা চাটা' (literally, foot-licking). The central conflict arises from the moral decay inherent in the court where flatterers, the 'চাটুকার,' actively undermine genuine leadership through insincere praise and servile admiration. Imagery of hollow praise and bent backs dominates the scene, illustrating a society where genuine merit is suffocated by insincere adoration. The chapter leaves the reader questioning the true cost of political maneuvering: can authentic leadership survive when the foundations of power are built solely upon the veneer of insincere flattery?
