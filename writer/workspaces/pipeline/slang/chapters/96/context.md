## chapter_summary

The chapter, titled 'হাত সাফ করা' (Clearing the Hand), delves into the philosophy that 'proverb is poetry,' exploring the swift and decisive nature of action when opportunity arises. The central conflict is the tension between deliberate, measured thought and the urgent necessity of seizing a fleeting chance, symbolized by the 'clean work' that demands immediate execution. Key imagery revolves around the swiftness of the 'clearing hand'—a metaphorical action signifying efficient, no-nonsense completion. As the characters navigate this moment of critical expediency, the narrative leaves the reader questioning whether this rapid, opportunistic success is truly a mark of wisdom or merely a calculated gamble against the slow grind of patience.
