## chapter_summary

The chapter, titled 'জগাখিচুড়ি' (Jogakhichuri), plunges the narrative into a state of absolute, chaotic disarray, serving as a microcosm of the larger political and social upheaval gripping the region. The central conflict manifests not as a grand battle, but as a frustrating, pervasive organizational breakdown—a 'mess' where every task, every plan, and every interaction devolves into a tangled, unproductive jumble. Key imagery revolves around the visual and operational chaos: piles of misfiled documents, overlapping responsibilities, and the cacophony of conflicting directives. As the characters navigate this labyrinth of incompetence, the lingering question hangs heavy: can this foundational disorder be salvaged, or is the current state of utter fragmentation a permanent reflection of the era's inherent instability?
