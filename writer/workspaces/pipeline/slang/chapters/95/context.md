## chapter_summary

Chapter 95, "বুকে পাষাণ বাঁধা" (Stone in the Chest), delves into the profound internal conflict faced by the protagonist, whose heart has hardened into a resolute, unyielding stone in preparation for the arduous journey ahead. This hardening symbolizes a necessary sacrifice, a stoic acceptance of duty that demands the abandonment of personal softness for the sake of a greater, perhaps grim, future. The imagery of the 'stone in the chest' serves as a stark metaphor for the emotional armor required to navigate the coming trials, transforming personal vulnerability into unwavering resolve. As the character prepares to leave, the narrative leaves the reader pondering whether this necessary emotional calcification will ultimately preserve them or irrevocably diminish the essence of who they once were.
