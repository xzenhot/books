## chapter_summary

The chapter, titled 'আমতা আমতা করা,' delves into the suffocating atmosphere of evasiveness, where the subtle dance of deceit becomes the prevailing social currency. The central conflict revolves around a character's agonizing indecision—the hesitation to speak the truth, choosing instead the perilous path of 'মিথ্যের তোতলামি' (the rambling of lies). Key imagery is painted through the nervous trembling of the character, whose very hesitation serves as an unspoken confession, a visible manifestation of guilt. As the narrative progresses through this web of unspoken truths and feigned uncertainty, the reader is left to question: Will this persistent pattern of self-doubt and evasion finally force a painful confrontation, or will the silence itself become the permanent, corrosive state of their existence?

- Focusing on the difference between cultural 'courtesy' (using circumlocution) and genuine evasion, charting where this line breaks in high-stakes interactions.
