## chapter_summary

Chapter 58, "হ্যাঁজানো," delves into the pervasive conflict between eloquence and substance, illustrating how excessive, needless chatter—the 'বিরক্তিকর বকবক'—can obscure the core message. The chapter employs the imagery of tangled threads or overgrown vines, symbolizing the way unnecessary elaboration buries the essential truth, making the intended meaning difficult to grasp. As characters engage in lengthy, meandering discussions, the narrative questions the true value of ornate speech when it lacks a clear, potent point, leaving the reader to ponder whether polish can ever truly replace profound simplicity.
