## chapter_summary

The chapter, titled 'ঘাটে ঘাটে জল খাওয়া,' delves into the harsh wisdom encapsulated in the proverb, 'পোড় খাওয়া অভিজ্ঞ — যে সব ঘাটের জল খেয়েছে, তাকে ঠকানো যায় না।' This segment explores the central conflict between ingrained, hard-won experience and the naive attempts of others to exploit or deceive those who have endured life's inevitable hardships. The imagery is steeped in the gritty reality of the river ghats—places of communal struggle, endurance, and unavoidable suffering—where every drop of water carries the residue of past trials. As the characters navigate this landscape of tested resilience, the narrative leaves the reader questioning: Does the depth of one's suffering truly equate to an impenetrable shield against all forms of deceit, or is there a vulnerability even the most seasoned soul cannot escape?
