## chapter_summary

The chapter, titled 'চোখে ধুলো দেওয়া' (To put dust in the eyes), plunges into a tense confrontation where cunning deception becomes the primary weapon. The central conflict revolves around a calculated act of evasion, as the protagonist employs elaborate stratagems to successfully mislead the pursuing police force. Key imagery is woven through the narrative—the dust, the fog of misdirection, and the shadows of deceit—as the character orchestrates a masterful escape. This episode leaves the reader pondering the true cost of such cleverness: whether this temporary triumph of wit over authority will ultimately secure freedom or merely postpone an inevitable reckoning.

### angles

- Focusing on the physical sensation of the dust itself—its grit, its color, how it lodges in a throat or catches the light at dawn.
- Framing the act not as successful evasion, but as an exhausting maintenance of illusion; where the performance requires more energy than confrontation would have.
- Treating 'Police' (C2) not as monolithic authority, but as individuals whose rigidity and procedural expectations make them susceptible to subtle semantic traps.
