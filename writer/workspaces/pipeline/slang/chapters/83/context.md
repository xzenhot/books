## chapter_summary

The chapter, titled 'ঘুঘু' (Dove/Pigeon), delves into a tense narrative where the central conflict revolves around the unseen cunning of a sly hunter. The imagery is dominated by the subtle, almost invisible traps set by this shrewd figure, suggesting a delicate dance of wits rather than brute force. The narrative explores the ambiguity of who is setting the snare and for what purpose, casting the entire interaction in a shadow of calculated deceit. As the chapter concludes, the reader is left pondering the ultimate nature of the game: is the hunter truly the master strategist, or is the perceived trap merely a reflection of a deeper, unknown vulnerability within the system itself?

### angles

- Framing 'ঘুঘু' not as a target but as an unintended consequence of the hunter's own flawed, overly complex strategy—the trap designed for others springs back onto the setter.
- Exploring the concept of shared vulnerability: The 'hunter' and the 'ঘুঘু' are both caught in the same ecosystem of suspicion; their interaction is less predator/prey and more two desperate survivors assessing risk.
- A study of inherited wisdom vs. learned cunning: Is the hunter relying on generations of rural survival tactics, making his traps inevitable rather than brilliant?
