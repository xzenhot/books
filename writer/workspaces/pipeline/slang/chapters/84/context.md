## chapter_title

## chapter_summary

The chapter delves into the subtle, unspoken rules of the powerful circles surrounding the office, personified by the 'ঘাটের পোকা'—the seasoned insiders of the local hub. The central conflict revolves around the delicate dance of patronage and influence, where knowledge of 'who to oil' (কারো কাছে তেল দিতে হয়) dictates one's standing and success, contrasting sharply with the more abstract realm of 'অন্তর্জ্ঞান' (intuitive wisdom). The narrative paints a vivid picture of this transactional social ecosystem, where street-smart pragmatism trumps mere intellectual understanding. As the characters navigate these murky waters, the reader is left questioning whether true merit can ever supersede the necessity of navigating the political currents of unspoken favors.
