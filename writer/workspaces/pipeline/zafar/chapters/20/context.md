### story_options

- Setting: A solitary lamp cast into a newly leveled patch of earth at dawn; Desire: To whisper a question—a single, profoundly personal query that has no place in imperial documentation—into the void before it is absorbed by the dust; Obstacle: The cold logic of the rising sun demanding perfect conformity.
