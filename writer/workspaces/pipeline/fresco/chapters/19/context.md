## creative_enrichment

### angles

- The sketch as a palimpsest: layers of erased intention visible under the desert light.
- Focusing not on loss, but on the potential energy trapped within the remnants—what it *almost* was.
- The physical interaction between the paper/support and the fine sand carried by the wind, turning artistic failure into geological process.

### story_options

- setting: By the outer edge of the haveli wall, desire: To find a pattern in the destruction—a signature left by the wind itself., obstacle: The sketches are scattered across dry, cracked earth below her scaffold., turn: She realizes the dispersal mirrors a complex geometric shape, an accidental motif.
- setting: Inside a discarded tin container used for pigment storage, desire: To find one single sketch miraculously protected from the elements., obstacle: The sketch is brittle and fused with dried lapis dust, unreadable by light., turn: She uses her tongue to moisten the edges, revealing a faint, almost forgotten instruction or symbol.

### subject_rotations

- Treating 'failure' not as artistic inadequacy, but as a necessary precursor that allows for the eventual perfection of the fresco.
- Examining the sketchbook/scaffolding material itself—the texture of the paper reacting to the harsh climate rather than just depicting art upon it.
- The wind as an active, intelligent agent that curates her failures, selecting only what is aesthetically interesting in its dispersal.

### sensory_details

- The brittle *shush* sound of dried charcoal fragments skittering across the sandstone base during a slight breeze.
- The metallic tang on the lips from inhaling dust mixed with fine gypsum powder after touching the debris.
- A sudden, cool shadow cast by the haveli's eaves falling over the remnants of colored chalk.

### research_questions

- What were common materials for preliminary studies or sketching before painting on wet lime surfaces in this region?

### structural_variants

- A highly fragmented, collage-like poem where each stanza is a different fragment of the lost sketch.
- Starting with a fully realized image (the 'blossom') and then methodically dismantling it through lines of erasure until only dust remains.

### unused_angles

- The contrast between the transient nature of paper/pencil work versus the stone permanence she seeks in fresco.
- A focus on the specific, minute biological life—insects or spores—that might inhabit and consume the discarded drafts before they are swept away.

### variety_note

This poem shifts focus from Debolina's external labor (scaffolding, lime) to her internal process of iteration, dwelling in the physical residues of things that did not succeed.
