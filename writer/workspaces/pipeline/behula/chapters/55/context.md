### angles

- Addressing the silence of an unheard listener (the wall/reader) throughout, forcing a half-conversation in the poem.
- Focusing on the metrical instability—allowing the rhythm to fracture precisely when hope is most fragile before settling into resolution.
- Contrasting the internal metaphysical triumph with the persistent grime and weariness of the physical domestic space.

### story_options

- A scene where she addresses a cracked piece of pottery, asking it if it witnessed the turning point.
- A hypothetical moment during the reunion where both characters pause, caught in the dissonance between their profound love and the harsh societal scrutiny they faced (The Wall).
- An internal monologue focusing on the weight of unspoken words—those she never uttered even to him, now confronting his return.

### subject_rotations

- Shifting focus from 'achievement' (winning) to 'endurance as a fundamental state' (simply continuing to exist against all odds).
- Treating the reunion not as an event but as the quiet negotiation of two perfectly altered selves.
- Examining how the *language* used during their separation has fundamentally changed them both.

- The gritty texture of dust motes dancing in a newly found shaft of morning light, symbolizing clarity emerging from confusion (Visual/Tactile).
- A profound, almost viscous silence that precedes his arrival, heavier than any noise (Auditory).
- The faint scent of aged, forgotten spices clinging to the thresholds, representing time suspended between crises (Olfactory).
