The world loudly worships truth while quietly rewarding fraud, and those who preach ethics most fervently are often the ones who break it most ruthlessly, leaving society to pay the price.

The powerful turn morality into a stage performance—reciting principles in public while shredding them in private. Their fraud is not hidden; it is normalized, even admired, because it succeeds. Meanwhile truth becomes a powerless spectator, praised but never protected. The real tragedy is not that lies exist, but that society keeps trusting the very people who betray it, mistaking eloquence for integrity.
