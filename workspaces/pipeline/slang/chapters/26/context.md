## chapter_title

## chapter_summary

The chapter delves into the pervasive atmosphere of idle gossip and petty machinations, encapsulated by the phrase 'proverb is poetry,' illustrating how seemingly harmless talk spirals into unproductive conflict. The central conflict revolves around the draining nature of these 'gultani'—small, time-wasting disputes that consume the characters' energy and focus without achieving any tangible goal. Key imagery is drawn from the stagnant, unproductive nature of this social malaise, where the potential for meaningful action is choked by endless chatter. As the characters navigate this web of trivialities, the lingering question remains: can they break free from this cycle of unproductive gossip, or is this petty drama the inescapable fabric of their daily existence?
