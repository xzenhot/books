## chapter_summary

The chapter, titled 'কাঁটা দিয়ে কাঁটা তোলা' (Pulling thorns with thorns), plunges into a grim strategic confrontation where the principle of 'enemy by enemy' is brutally enacted, mirroring the imagery of extinguishing a fire with fire. The central conflict revolves around a desperate, retaliatory maneuver, where the protagonist must utilize methods mirroring those of their adversaries to achieve a necessary, albeit morally fraught, victory. The atmosphere is thick with the heat of conflict, symbolized by the relentless imagery of conflagration and scorched earth. As the tactical maneuvering reaches its perilous climax, the narrative leaves the reader questioning the true cost of such necessary ruthlessness: can a victory achieved through mirroring malice ever truly cleanse the stain of conflict?
