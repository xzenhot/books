## chapter_title

## chapter_summary

The chapter plunges into a crisis of trust as the protagonist confronts the devastating reality of a guardian's betrayal. The central conflict is starkly illuminated by the proverb, 'When the field eats, I wander'—a metaphor for a time of profound vulnerability where external forces, symbolized by the encroaching fields, threaten stability. Key imagery revolves around the precarious balance between the protagonist's solitude and the insidious nature of the deceit, casting long shadows over their perceived safety. This betrayal forces a deep internal reckoning, leaving the reader to question: In a world where the protector turns into a threat, can any allegiance be truly trusted?

- Examining the field not as an external predator, but as a reflection of C1's own internal barrenness or stagnation.
- Framing the betrayal through a purely transactional lens: What exactly did the 'guardian' gain from causing this vulnerability?
- Deconstructing the proverb by focusing on the *sound*—the way 'খেত খাওয়া' rolls off the tongue compared to other, harsher slang phrases.

- Shifting focus entirely to C2’s perception of the guardian, examining bias and pre-existing suspicion rather than the objective reality of betrayal.
