### angles

- Treating 'morning' not as a time of arrival, but as the moment potential energy transforms into kinetic uncertainty—the vibration before movement.
- Framing the light source as inherently conditional: it is only *appearing*, never fully manifested, suggesting that hope itself requires constant projection to sustain.
- A focus on sonic residue within the dawn; the last echo of night's struggle clinging to the first quiet moments of day.

### subject_rotations

- Examining the *delay* inherent in new light—the interval between belief and tangible confirmation.
- Shifting from 'hope' as an active drive, to hope as a passive, receptive state that must be maintained through vigilance against doubt.
- Analyzing the boundary of dawn not as a line, but as a permeability—how shadows bleed into potential, rather than simply disappearing.
