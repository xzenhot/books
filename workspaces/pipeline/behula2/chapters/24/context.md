### angles

- Treating the inner voice not as an oracle offering a solution, but as a highly biased internal debate—a series of conflicting 'shoulds' vying for dominance.
- Framing the 'voice' as a resonance frequency that only emerges when external noise levels drop to a critical threshold, making silence a necessary physical apparatus.
- Shifting focus from *hearing* the voice to the *physical act* of trying to impose a clear cadence onto disorganized internal monologue.

- Re-evaluating the symbol of the 'light' as not an illumination, but a subtle internal bioluminescence visible only during extreme mental strain.
